Level Up 2 · How apps really work
New word unlocked
Vibe coding
You describe what you want in normal language. The AI writes the code. You test it and steer.
The name was coined by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy in February 2025. Today it’s how lots of people build their very first app.
How it works
The vibe coding loop
Describe
Say clearly what you want.
Generate
The AI writes or changes the code.
Test
Click everything. Try to break it.
Refine
Say exactly what to fix or improve.
…then back to Describe. Real builders go round this loop dozens of times. Your first version is never the final one.
Level Up 2 · How apps really work
Your job
You’re the director, not the passenger
Does it work?
Click every button. Type silly things. Try it on a phone.
Is it safe?
No real personal data. No secrets. Nothing anyone could misuse.
Is it what I wanted?
If not, say exactly what’s different. “Make it better” isn’t an instruction.
If you can’t explain what your app does, you’re not finished.

Level Up 2 · How apps really work
Once you’re building
Pro moves for building with AI
One change at a time
Small steps are easier to check and to undo.
Report bugs like a detective
What I did → what I expected → what happened.
Show, don’t just tell
Add a screenshot, or name an app whose look you like.
Ask for the plan first
“Before you change anything, explain what you’ll do.”
Go back in time
Badly broken? Restore an earlier version instead of patching.
Ask “why?”
Ask the AI to explain its code simply. That’s how you learn.

Before anyone builds
The builder’s code
No real personal data. No classmates’ names, photos or phone numbers.
No passwords or secret keys in a prompt. Ever.
AI inside your app? Assume someone will try to trick it.
Build things that help. No apps that rate, rank or make fun of anyone.
School accounts only. Nothing you wouldn’t show your teacher.
